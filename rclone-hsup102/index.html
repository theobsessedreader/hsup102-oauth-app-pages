Rclone - HSUP102

Purpose: A personal-use Google OAuth client that permits the account holder to connect their Google Drive to Rclone on an Ubuntu workstation.

Rclone is file-management software that can list, access, download, upload, and manage cloud files, subject to the Google account permissions granted during sign-in. The integration is intended for the owner's own Google accounts, not as a public service or a shared file-hosting application.

The OAuth sign-in is performed with Google, and the resulting authorization is used by Rclone on the owner's computer. This informational website does not request credentials or receive Google Drive content.

Privacy and data use

Please read the Rclone - HSUP102 privacy policy for details about Google Drive permissions, local storage, and revoking access.

This is a personal Rclone configuration, not an official Google or Rclone product. For questions, visit the repository owner's GitHub profile.
